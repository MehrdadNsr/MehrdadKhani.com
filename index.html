01 / About

Purification, from the bench to the batch record.

I am a biotechnologist with six years in GMP-regulated biopharmaceutical and pharmaceutical manufacturing, about three and a half of them in recombinant protein expression and purification across mammalian (CHO, HEK), insect (Sf9) and bacterial (E. coli) systems.

At CinnaGen I ran multistep chromatography trains (Protein A, IEX, HIC, SEC) on ÄKTA Pilot and Process systems, viral inactivation and nanofiltration, TFF and UF/DF, and documented every run in UNICORN and batch records. I later moved into process and cleaning validation in QA, and then to Canada, where I spent two and a half years in chemical API production at Eurofins CDMO Alphora across five suites and batch scales from 5 L to 400 L.

I hold a WES-certified BSc in Biotechnology and the BioReady Process Development Technician certification from BioTalent Canada. I am looking for a downstream, bioprocess or manufacturing role in Canada where that mix of hands-on purification, validation and cross-functional coordination is useful on day one.
